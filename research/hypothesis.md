# Hypotheses and future operationalization
Primary: EEG activity during imagined left- and right-hand movement contains distinguishable patterns that can be classified above chance using machine-learning methods.

Secondary: Features associated with sensorimotor scalp regions will contribute more strongly to classification than features from unrelated regions.

Future primary endpoint: held-out balanced accuracy, with chance reference 0.5
for this binary task. Above-chance evidence will require a prespecified validation
scheme and permutation test preserving run structure, not just a score over 0.5.

Future regional comparison: prespecify sensorimotor channels (e.g. C3, Cz, C4 and
adjacent central electrodes), comparison channels and matched channel counts before
examining predictive performance. Compare held-out regional ablations and report
uncertainty. Channel importance and CSP patterns do not establish causal contribution
or localize neural sources. No hypothesis has been tested in Phase 1.

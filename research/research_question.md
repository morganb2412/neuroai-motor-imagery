# Research question
Can classical machine-learning models distinguish imagined left-hand from right-hand movement in EEG, and which EEG features contribute most strongly?

## Primary hypothesis
EEG activity during imagined left- and right-hand movement contains distinguishable patterns that can be classified above chance using machine-learning methods.

## Secondary hypothesis
Features associated with sensorimotor scalp regions will contribute more strongly to classification than features from unrelated regions.

## Research domain
NeuroAI, noninvasive EEG, motor imagery and classical neural decoding.

## Dataset
PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset, version 1.0.0.

## Initial experimental goals
Retrieve and inspect subject 1; verify imagery run/event meanings; filter, epoch,
count and visualize trials. Establish reproducible provenance before testing hypotheses.
Phase 1 does not test classification or feature contribution.
